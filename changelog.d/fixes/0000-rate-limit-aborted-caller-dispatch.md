- **fix(rate-limit):** `withRateLimit` no longer starts the scheduled work of a caller that was aborted while it waited for its provider slot. With limiter capacity free, Bottleneck dispatches the job before `abandonQueuedJob()` can pull it from the queue, so the work ran with an already-aborted signal; work that only listens for a later `abort` event then held the limiter slot and Bottleneck's ref'd expiration timer for `executionMaxWaitMs` (10 min by default). The dispatched job now rejects without calling the work, as it already did after a queue-wait timeout, and `tests/unit/rate-limit-manager.test.ts` exits right after its last test instead of about 10 minutes later.

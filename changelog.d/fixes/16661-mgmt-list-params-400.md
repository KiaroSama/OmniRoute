- **fix(api):** the list routes of the dashboard and the management API (`/api/batches`, `/api/files`, `/api/logs/detail`, `/api/cache/entries`, `/api/webhooks`, `/api/v1/management/proxies` and `/api/v1/management/proxies/assignments`) parsed `?limit=`, `?offset=` and `?page=` with a bare `Number`/`Number.parseInt`; a value such as `abc` reached the SQLite `LIMIT ?` bind as `NaN` (`datatype mismatch`) and the request ended as a 500 or threw out of the handler. They now answer 400 `<name> must be an integer` through one helper, as `/v1/batches` and `/v1/files` already did for `limit`; out-of-range integers are still clamped as before ([#16661](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/16661)) — thanks @pacocartones.
